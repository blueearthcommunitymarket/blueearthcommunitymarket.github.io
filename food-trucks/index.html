Asian-inspired street food. Wednesdays and select Saturdays.

Egg Rolls Crab Rangoons Pork Kabobs Beef Broccoli Orange Chicken Fried Rice

Cash or card accepted ($1 fee). Gift certificates available.

Bummy's BBQ ↗  Facebook ↗

BBQ out of Kiester, MN. First Tuesday of every month, April through September.

Cheezers ↗

A food truck built around one simple thing: cheesy comfort food. Tuesdays starting mid-May through September, plus special events.

Cheesy Comfort Food

Hungry Boys ↗

Kris Tacos ↗

Authentic Mexican street food. Mondays and Fridays all season long.

Tacos Quesadillas Burritos Nachos Tortas Quesa Birra Spiral Potato Elote En Vaso Mangonatas

507-525-8243 / 507-822-7319  |  We take orders

Sip Haus ↗

A mobile dirty soda and lemonade bar serving Southern Minnesota. Bringing a fun, elevated drink experience to events. Featured at the Ryno's Rock N' Roll-Ins event June 19.

Dirty Sodas Lemonade

The Lemon ↗

Fresh squeezed lemonade — lemonade the way it's supposed to be! Featured at the Hi Way Motorfest event September 13.

Original Strawberry Raspberry Mango Watermelon White Peach

Special Events

Ryno's Rock N' Roll-Ins are live music events at the Ryno Food Lot. Drive in style — side-by-sides, golf carts, and four-wheelers welcome. Bring a lawn chair and hang out. Music, machines, community.
May 29 — The Lemon, Kris Tacos | Live Music: Morgan Dickman (6–9 PM)
June 19 — Sip Haus, Cheezers | Live Music: Blue Dirt Road
Hi Way Motorfest (Sept 13) — The Lemon and Cheezers.
