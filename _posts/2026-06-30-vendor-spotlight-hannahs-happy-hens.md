We’re happy to spotlight **Hannah's Happy Hens**, a farm-fresh booth bringing both chicken eggs and duck eggs to the Blue Earth Community Market.

Hannah shared that one of the best parts of market day is seeing friends, community members, and students. That local connection is exactly the kind of energy we want the market to keep growing.

Along with fresh eggs, Hannah is also planning to bring **ducklings later this summer**, which makes this one of those booths families and regular shoppers will want to keep an eye on.

We’re glad to have Hannah's Happy Hens in the market lineup and hope you’ll stop by to say hello and pick up some farm-fresh goods.
