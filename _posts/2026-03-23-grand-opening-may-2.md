Mark your calendars! The Blue Earth Community Market opens for the 2026 season on **Saturday, May 2nd**.

We'll be at 112 S Grove St in Blue Earth from **10:00am to 1:00pm**. Come shop local, meet your neighbors, and kick off another great market season.

We're welcoming back many familiar vendors and some exciting new faces. More announcements coming soon as we finalize the vendor lineup.

**Market details:**
- Every Saturday, 10am-1pm
- May 2 through October 2026
- 112 S Grove St, Blue Earth, MN
- Free admission
- Leashed pets welcome

Follow us on [Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/share/1BiTzAtMjP/?mibextid=wwXIfr) for the latest vendor announcements and updates. See you May 2nd!
