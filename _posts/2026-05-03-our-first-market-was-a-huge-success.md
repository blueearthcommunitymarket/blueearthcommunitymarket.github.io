From the bottom of our hearts, thank you to our amazing vendors and community for showing up in such a big way for our first market of the season.

We could not have asked for a better opener. More than five vendors sold out of their products, some within the first 30 minutes, and the whole morning was filled with energy, support, and excitement for what this season can become.

Our May 2 market featured an incredible mix of local vendors, including Blue Earth Cooperative Bakery, Ghostly Paracord, Alluring Tranquility, Vitas Tamales, Bread and Book Bakery, Cookies Bars and Jars, The One n Only Sweet Spot, Steele Family Farm, Little Ryno Stand, River Shark Books, Prop-Able, Unique Items by April, Little Reds Seasoning, Beautiful Brushstrokes, and Sassy Sprouts Homestead.

We are also going to keep working hard to create more booth spaces so we can welcome even more vendors in the weeks ahead.

Thank you again to everyone who helped make opening day such a success. We are so excited for what is ahead this season.
