We’re excited to spotlight **Ghostly Paracord**, a creative handmade vendor bringing durable paracord goods to the Blue Earth Community Market.

<img src="/assets/images/vendors/ghostly-paracord.png" alt="Ghostly Paracord at Blue Earth Community Market" style="max-width: 420px; width: 100%; height: auto; display: block; margin: 1rem auto; border-radius: 12px;">

Created by **Christion Cromwell**, Ghostly Paracord offers handcrafted keychains, lanyards, phone cords, zip pulls, and dog leashes. Each piece blends practical use with a handmade touch, which makes this booth a fun stop for anyone who likes useful everyday items with a little personality.

We love the range here. Some pieces are simple and functional, and others make easy gifts or small add-ons while you shop the rest of the market.

If you are coming to opening day, keep an eye out for Ghostly Paracord and see what Christion has on hand.
