We’re happy to spotlight **River Shark Books**, a local author booth that adds stories, creativity, and conversation to market day.

<img src="/assets/images/vendors/river-shark-books.png" alt="River Shark Books at Blue Earth Community Market" style="max-width: 420px; width: 100%; height: auto; display: block; margin: 1rem auto; border-radius: 12px;">

**Danielle Hayter-Johnson** brings River Shark Books to the Blue Earth Community Market with a lineup centered on books, literary work, and the chance to meet the author behind them.

We love the mix this brings to opening day. A market is always stronger when it includes makers of all kinds, and it is especially fun to have books and original writing alongside baked goods, handmade items, and farm products.

If you enjoy discovering local authors or picking up something new to read, make time to stop by River Shark Books on May 2.
