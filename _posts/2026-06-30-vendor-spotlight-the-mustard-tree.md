We’re glad to spotlight **The Mustard Tree**, a small-batch vendor bringing spiced honey, herbal teas, and handcrafted salves to the Blue Earth Community Market.

Owner **Erin Hinkle** builds each offering with care, using local ingredients and traditional methods to create products that feel thoughtful, practical, and a little different from the usual market table.

The Mustard Tree adds a nice mix of pantry, wellness, and gift-friendly items to the market. If you like discovering small-batch goods with a homemade feel, this is a booth worth visiting.

We’re happy to have The Mustard Tree as part of the market community, and we hope you’ll stop by to see what Erin is bringing this season.
