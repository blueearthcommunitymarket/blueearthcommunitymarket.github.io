We have another local musician lined up for the Blue Earth Community Market open mic.

**Rob Milton** is scheduled to play on **Saturday, July 25** from **10:30 AM to 11:30 AM** during market at the Ryno Community Lot.

Rob's signup included a set list mix of familiar singalong favorites and classic crowd-pleasers, including:

- *Breakfast at Tiffany's*
- *Ghost Riders in the Sky*
- *Fields of Gold*
- *Beautiful World*
- *Collide*
- *Wash*
- *The Gambler*
- *All In*
- *4 Chords*
- *Good Riddance (Time of Your Life)*

He also noted that he will be bringing his own instruments, mics, amps, stands, cords, stool, and PA, and power can be accommodated on site if needed.

Open mic runs during the community market and is always **weather permitting**. If the market has to move indoors, open mic will not run that day.

Come shop local, hang out for a while, and catch a live set while you are at the market.

Want to grab your own slot too? Visit the [Open Mic page](/open-mic/).
