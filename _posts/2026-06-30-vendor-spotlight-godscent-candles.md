We’re excited to spotlight **GodScent Candles**, a handmade candle business bringing warm, giftable home fragrance products to the Blue Earth Community Market.

Owner **Emma Lawrence** makes soy wax candles and wax melts designed to be **clean-burning, non-toxic, and pet friendly**, which makes this booth a great fit for shoppers looking for cozy scents and thoughtful gifts.

GodScent Candles adds a polished home-fragrance option to the market mix, with small-batch products that feel both practical and special.

We’re glad to have GodScent Candles as part of the market community, and we hope you’ll stop by to see what scents Emma is bringing to the booth.
