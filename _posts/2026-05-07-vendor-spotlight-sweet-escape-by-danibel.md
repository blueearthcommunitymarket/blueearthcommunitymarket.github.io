We’re happy to spotlight **Sweet Escape by Danibel**, one of the newer vendor names customers will see at the Blue Earth Community Market.

Sweet Escape by Danibel is its own business, even though it signs up and sets up alongside **Vitas Tamales**. That means market visitors can look forward to a separate dessert focused booth as part of the lineup.

We love having booths that pair naturally together, and this is a great example. Savory favorites from one table and sweet treats from the next make for a pretty great market stop.

If you are coming to the market this week, keep an eye out for Sweet Escape by Danibel and give this newly featured vendor a warm welcome.
