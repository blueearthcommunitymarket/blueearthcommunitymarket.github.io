We’re excited to spotlight **Dana's Mexican Food /Snacks**, a new prepared food vendor joining the Blue Earth Community Market lineup.

Prepared food always adds energy to the market, and Dana's Mexican Food /Snacks brings a menu that feels fun, colorful, and easy to enjoy while you shop.

Watch for Dana's Mexican Food /Snacks at upcoming market dates and be ready to stop by hungry.
