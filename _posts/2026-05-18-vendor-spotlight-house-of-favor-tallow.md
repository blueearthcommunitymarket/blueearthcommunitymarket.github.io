We’re happy to spotlight **House of Favor Tallow**, a small-batch skincare business bringing handcrafted tallow-based products to the Blue Earth Community Market.

Owner **Beth Celander** created House of Favor Tallow after seeing how nourishing tallow skincare could be for her own family. What started as personal use while caring for sensitive skin grew into a business centered on restoration, simplicity, and intentional care.

House of Favor Tallow offers whipped tallow and balm-style skincare made with locally rendered beef tallow, natural oils, and carefully selected essential oils. Beth says one of the most popular scents to try first is **Joyful**, a warm citrus-spice blend that customers describe as softening, nourishing, and refreshing. She also offers unscented options for sensitive skin.

What makes this business stand out is the hands-on process behind every batch. Beth personally renders and purifies the tallow she uses, then combines traditional skincare ingredients with a modern natural approach.

Beth has strong ties to southern Minnesota and loves being part of local events that connect people face-to-face. We’re glad to welcome House of Favor Tallow to the market community and excited for shoppers to discover this thoughtful line of handcrafted skincare.
