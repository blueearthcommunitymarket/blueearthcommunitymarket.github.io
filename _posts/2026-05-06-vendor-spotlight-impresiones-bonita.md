We’re excited to spotlight **Impresiones Bonita**, a new bakery vendor bringing rich homemade cakes and desserts to the Blue Earth Community Market.

This booth features favorites like **tres leches**, **chocoflan**, and **Beso de Ángel y tres leches**, making it a great stop for anyone with a sweet tooth.

We love the variety Impresiones Bonita adds to the market. A table full of celebration style desserts brings a different flavor to market morning, and it gives visitors one more reason to walk the whole lineup.

Be sure to stop by Impresiones Bonita and see what sweet treats are headed to market this week.
