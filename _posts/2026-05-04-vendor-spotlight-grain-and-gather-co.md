We’re glad to spotlight **Grain & Gather Co**, a new bakery vendor joining the Blue Earth Community Market community.

Owned by **Katie Gustafson**, Grain & Gather Co brings small batch baked goods with cottage bakery roots and a love for market day treats. Katie shared that she recently moved from Michigan and already has cottage food experience, which makes this a fun new addition to the market lineup.

We always love welcoming another baker into the mix, especially one focused on handmade, small batch goods. It is the kind of booth that makes market mornings smell even better.

Watch for Grain & Gather Co at future market dates, and we hope you will give this new vendor a warm Blue Earth welcome.
