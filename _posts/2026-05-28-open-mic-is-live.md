Open mic is officially live at the Ryno Community Lot.

If you have been wanting to play a few songs during market, now you can claim a spot. Open mic runs during the Saturday community market from **10:00am to 1:00pm** in **30-minute slots**, weather permitting.

This is meant to feel easy and welcoming. Singers, pickers, bands, and first-timers are all welcome as long as the music stays family friendly and fits the community market atmosphere.

## What to Know

- **Sign up online:** [Open Mic Signup](/open-mic/)
- **Slots run every Saturday** during the community market, weather permitting
- **Electricity may be available**, but performers need to bring their own instruments, mics, stands, cords, and other gear
- **Want a longer set?** You can claim more than one slot
- **Need to cancel?** Please cancel before **Monday at 10:00 PM**. After that, signup for that Saturday is closed

If the market has to move indoors for bad weather, open mic will not run that day.

You can grab a time slot on the [Open Mic page](/open-mic/), and after that, fill out the quick performer form so we know what kind of music you are bringing and whether you need electricity.

We are excited to hear what the community brings to the lot. See you on a Saturday morning.
