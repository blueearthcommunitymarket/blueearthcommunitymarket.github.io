We’re excited to spotlight **Cookies Bars & Jars**, a Blue Earth vendor bringing a homemade mix of breads, treats, and canned goods to opening day.

<img src="/assets/images/vendors/cookies-bars-and-jars.png" alt="Cookies Bars and Jars at Blue Earth Community Market" style="max-width: 420px; width: 100%; height: auto; display: block; margin: 1rem auto; border-radius: 12px;">

Owned by **Kathy Anderson**, Cookies Bars & Jars offers sourdough breads, yeasted breads, cookies, bars, and home canned goods. It is the kind of booth that feels rooted in comfort food, tradition, and the simple pleasure of taking something homemade home with you.

We love the variety here. Whether you are after a loaf of bread, a sweet treat, or something shelf-stable to enjoy later, this booth brings a little bit of everything.

Be sure to stop by on May 2 and see what Kathy has prepared for market day.
