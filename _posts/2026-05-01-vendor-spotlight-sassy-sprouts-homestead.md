We’re glad to spotlight **Sassy Sprouts Homestead**, a vendor bringing a warm mix of home and handmade goods to the Blue Earth Community Market.

Owned by **Stacy Hander**, Sassy Sprouts Homestead offers home canned goods, eggs, burp cloths, towels, and plush handmade stuffies. It is a booth with a real homestead feel, practical, personal, and full of items made with care.

We love the range here. Some visitors will come for pantry staples or eggs, and others will find something soft, useful, or giftable to bring home. That mix is part of what makes a community market fun to explore.

If you are joining us on May 2, make sure to stop by Sassy Sprouts Homestead and see what Stacy is bringing.
