If you are planning to shop the **Saturday, July 4** Blue Earth Community Market, **Goemann Produce** shared a fresh preview of what they expect to have at the booth this week.

Sarah Goemann says shoppers can look for **peas**, including **snow peas** and **sugar snap peas**, along with **free-range eggs**. She also expects to have **radishes**, though she noted she would know more for sure the next day.

It is exactly the kind of early-summer market lineup we love to see: fresh, local, and ready for the holiday weekend.

If Goemann Produce is on your Saturday stop list, come early and see what is ready at the table.
