If you have a sweet tooth, you will want to keep an eye out for **The One n Only Sweet Spot** at the Blue Earth Community Market.

Owned by **Amylia Alvear**, this bakery brings a delicious lineup of treats to market, including **cookies, cupcakes, brownies, cookie pies, and mini cakes**.

The One n Only Sweet Spot adds exactly the kind of homemade fun we love to see at the market: colorful baked goods, crowd-pleasing favorites, and plenty of reasons to stop for something sweet while you shop.

We are excited to have Amylia as part of the Blue Earth Community Market vendor community, and we hope you'll stop by her booth to pick out a favorite or try something new.

Sweet treats, local makers, and Saturdays in Blue Earth, that sounds like a pretty good combination to us.
