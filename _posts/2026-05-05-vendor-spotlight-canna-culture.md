We’re glad to spotlight **Canna Culture**, a new vendor bringing mushroom based products and specialty food items to the Blue Earth Community Market.

Owner **Amber Olsen** is rolling out a booth with plantable mushroom kits, infused mushroom honeys, dried mushrooms, drinks, and other unique products as the business continues to grow.

This is the kind of vendor that adds curiosity and conversation to market day. Whether you already love mushroom products or just want to see something new, Canna Culture will be a fun stop to explore.

Catch Canna Culture at the market this week and see what they are bringing to Blue Earth.
