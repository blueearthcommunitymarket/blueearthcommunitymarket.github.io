We’re proud to spotlight **Blue Earth Cooperative Bakery**, a weekly market favorite known for artisan bakes made right here in Blue Earth.

Led by **Heather McElyea-Chunn**, the bakery brings a lineup of small-batch sourdough breads, focaccia, scones, cookies, cakes, and seasonal kringla. Everything is handmade with care, and each market day gives visitors a chance to grab something fresh, local, and genuinely special.

Blue Earth Cooperative Bakery is also part of a bigger story. Bakery sales help support the preservation of the historic cooperative building tied to the business, which makes every loaf and every treat part of something meaningful in town.

We are grateful to have the bakery as part of the Blue Earth Community Market and excited to see what Heather brings for opening day.
