ROCKIN' RYNO MARKET TAKEOVER WEEK keeps bringing the flavor.

Today's featured act is **Vita's Tamales**, bringing traditional Mexican food, family recipes, and handmade tamales to the lot for a full weekend of community fun.

Owner **Ma E Raminez** built the business from the tamales her grandmother made for family celebrations. After moving from Mexico to Texas, she kept that tradition alive by cooking for family and friends, and over time that love of sharing good food grew into Vita's Tamales.

At the market, Vita's Tamales brings crowd-favorite flavors like **chicken, pork, cheese, and cheese with jalapeno**, along with other delicious traditional Mexican foods.
