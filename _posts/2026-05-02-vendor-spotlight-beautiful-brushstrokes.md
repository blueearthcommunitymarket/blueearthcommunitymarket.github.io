We’re happy to spotlight **Beautiful Brushstrokes**, one of the creative vendors joining us for opening day at the Blue Earth Community Market.

**Charlotte Bach** brings artful handmade goods and creative local pieces to the market. While we are still waiting on a little more of her featured vendor info, we wanted to make sure she is part of the opening day lineup here on the site too.

Markets are always better when they include artists and makers alongside bakers and growers, and we are glad Beautiful Brushstrokes will be part of the mix on May 2.

Come by, say hello, and see what Charlotte brings to market day.
