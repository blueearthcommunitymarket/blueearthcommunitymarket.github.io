We’re happy to spotlight **Steele Family Farm**, one of the vendors joining us for opening day with farm fresh goods and more to come later in the season.

Owner **Amanda Steele** is bringing **eggs** for the May 2 market, with **succulents** also planned as the season develops. We always love having booths that reflect the rhythms of the growing season, and Steele Family Farm is a great fit for that.

Fresh eggs are one of those simple market staples people are always glad to find, and we are excited to welcome Amanda to the Blue Earth Community Market lineup.

If you are shopping opening day, be sure to stop by Steele Family Farm and say hello.
