We’re glad to spotlight **Unique Items by April**, a booth full of creative finds and fun variety for opening day at the Blue Earth Community Market.

<img src="/assets/images/vendors/unique-items-by-april.png" alt="Unique Items by April at Blue Earth Community Market" style="max-width: 420px; width: 100%; height: auto; display: block; margin: 1rem auto; border-radius: 12px;">

**April Florez** brings a mix of tumblers, 3D items, laser engraved goods, and freeze dried candy. It is a lineup that makes this booth especially fun to browse, because you never quite know what favorite item you might land on first.

We love vendors that bring a little surprise to the market, and Unique Items by April definitely fits that category. There is a playful mix here that should make this a popular stop on opening day.

If you are coming to market on May 2, make sure to swing by and see what April has in store.
