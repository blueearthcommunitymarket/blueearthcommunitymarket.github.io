What a beautiful day we had at the Blue Earth Community Market this past Saturday.

The market was full of happy energy, smiling faces, and so much community support from start to finish. It was one of those mornings that reminded us exactly why we love bringing people together in downtown Blue Earth.

We are so grateful for everyone who came out to shop, visit, and enjoy the fun with us. Thank you for helping make it such a successful market day.

If this weekend was any sign of what is ahead, we have a lot to look forward to this season. We cannot wait to do it again next Saturday.
