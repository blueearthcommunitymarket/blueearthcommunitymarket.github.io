**Little Ryno Stand** is one of those booths that adds something extra to market day.

<img src="/assets/images/vendors/little-ryno-stand.png" alt="Little Ryno Stand at Blue Earth Community Market" style="max-width: 420px; width: 100%; height: auto; display: block; margin: 1rem auto; border-radius: 12px;">

Operated by **Morgan Myers** and the **Ryno girls**, this cheerful pink shed serves as a little community hub at the Blue Earth Community Market. You can stop by for **self-serve coffee, bottled water, popcorn, peanuts, market merch**, and even information about what is happening around the lot.

Morgan describes it as a kind of **grand central station** for the market, and that feels exactly right. It is also where visitors can find the **first aid kit**, which makes the stand both fun and genuinely helpful.

The story behind the stand is just as memorable. The shed started life as a gray pump house at a bin site near Huntley, then got a full makeover so the Ryno girls could have a place to sell whatever creative ideas they dreamed up for market season.

There is more to come, too. Along with their popular **Meet Me at the Market** stickers, Morgan shared that Devyn has plans for **DIY crafts** people can enjoy at the market.

We love the energy Little Ryno Stand brings to the Blue Earth Community Market, and we hope you stop by to say hello.
