We’re excited to spotlight **Little Reds Seasoning**, joining the Blue Earth Community Market for opening day with flavorful seasoning blends and a creative local touch.

<img src="/assets/images/vendors/little-reds-seasonings.png" alt="Little Reds Seasoning at Blue Earth Community Market" style="max-width: 420px; width: 100%; height: auto; display: block; margin: 1rem auto; border-radius: 12px;">

Owner **Kelly Bromeland** describes herself as the mastermind behind Little Reds Seasoning. She created the blend on her own with encouragement from friends and from her sister in Kentucky, then decided to take the leap and share it with more people.

Right now, Little Reds Seasoning features one signature blend: **Little Reds All Purpose Seasoning + Rub**. Kelly is excited to get it out into the world, especially because this whole seasoning adventure is brand new for her.

Kelly is also a mom of four boys, with three still at home, and she says she loves cooking for her husband **Eli**. That real love of feeding people makes this kind of business feel especially personal to us.

Along with her seasoning blend, Kelly will also be bringing a few painted items for purchase. She runs **Little Reds Seasoning** on Facebook and also has a separate page, **Twisted Bristle**, for paintings and art she has created over the years.

Kelly grew up in **Elmore** and now lives there with her family. We are glad to have her as part of the Blue Earth Community Market, and we are excited to see where this new venture goes next.

If you are coming to opening day, stop by and see what Kelly is bringing.
