We’re excited to spotlight **Little Reds Seasoning**, joining the Blue Earth Community Market for opening day with flavorful seasoning blends for home cooks.

<img src="/assets/images/vendors/little-reds-seasonings.png" alt="Little Reds Seasoning at Blue Earth Community Market" style="max-width: 420px; width: 100%; height: auto; display: block; margin: 1rem auto; border-radius: 12px;">

**Kelly Bromeland** brings a simple but very practical kind of product to the market: seasoning blends that make it easier to add flavor in everyday cooking. We love booths like this because they fit naturally into how people actually cook and share food at home.

If you enjoy trying something new in the kitchen or picking up small local staples that you will really use, Little Reds Seasoning will be a great stop on May 2.
