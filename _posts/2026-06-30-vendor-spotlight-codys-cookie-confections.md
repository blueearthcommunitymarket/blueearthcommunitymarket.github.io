We’re excited to spotlight **Cody's Cookie Confections**, a beach-themed cookie and collectible sticker brand bringing a fun mix of sweets, creativity, and character to the Blue Earth Community Market.

Owner **Cody Opheim** describes himself as the creator, baker, decorator, and designer behind the business. What started as a small creative outlet grew into a brand built around nostalgia, creativity, and community interaction.

Cody specializes in **custom decorated sugar cookies**, beach-themed treats, and collectible sticker products featuring his original **Cookie Cove** characters and designs. It is a playful booth with a strong visual style, and it adds something a little different to the market mix.

One of the things that stands out most is how quickly the brand has been growing. Cody shared that his products have already reached **7 states**, with that number expected to grow to **10 states by July**.

We’re glad to have Cody's Cookie Confections as part of the market community, and we hope you’ll stop by to see what sweet and creative items he brings to the booth.
