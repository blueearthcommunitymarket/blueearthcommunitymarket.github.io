Open Mic Signup

Singers, pickers, poets, storytellers, and brave first-timers are all welcome. Open mic runs during the Saturday community market, weather permitting, and stays outdoors only.

A simple way to get on the lineup

This page is here to make signup easy. Use SignUpGenius to claim your time slot, then use the short performer form below to send us your contact info and setup details.

Open mic runs every Saturday during the community market, weather permitting. If the market has to move indoors because of bad weather, open mic will not run that day.

Before you sign up, please make note of one important detail: electricity is available, but sound equipment is not. If you need power, tell us in your form so we can plan around it.

Open Mic Rules

  • Family friendly only. No hate speech, sexually explicit material, or content that would make the market uncomfortable for kids and families.
  • Weather permitting. Open mic is for outdoor market days only. If the market moves indoors for bad weather, open mic is canceled for that day.
  • 30-minute slots. Slots start at 10:00am and run every 30 minutes until 1:00pm.
  • Bring your own gear. Electricity may be available, but performers need to bring their own instruments, mics, stands, cords, and other equipment.
  • Keep it community friendly. The host may stop or decline a performance that does not fit the tone of the market.
