Good to Know

Market Day Details

  • Market runs rain or shine (indoor backup available for severe weather)
  • Leashed, well-behaved pets are welcome
  • Free admission for shoppers
  • No vendor fee for farmers, bakers, and makers
  • Food trucks welcome (city food service permit required)
