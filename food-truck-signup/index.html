Food Truck Permit Process

Operating a food truck in Blue Earth requires a city permit. Here's how to get one:

  1. Download and fill out the Solicitor, Peddler & Transient Merchant Application (PDF)
  2. Submit your application to City Hall
  3. Your application is reviewed and approved at the next city council meeting

The annual Transient Vendor Permit is $115/yr. Questions? Contact City Hall directly.
